# Contractor Job Profit & Quote System

## Positioning
A practical pricing and profitability workbook for independent contractors and service businesses who need to know what to charge before accepting a job.

## Core promise
Price jobs from true cost, overhead, and target margin instead of guessing from markup alone.

## Included
- Job Quote calculator
- true job cost calculation
- labor and material markup controls
- overhead reserve
- target net-margin pricing
- break-even price
- deposit and balance calculation
- +20% labor/material stress tests
- job-history tracker
- actual profit and margin tracking
- profit dashboard
