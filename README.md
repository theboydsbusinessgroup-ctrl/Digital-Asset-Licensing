# Digital Asset Licensing

Personal income-generation project. **Not for sale.**

## Mission
Build a low-maintenance, $0-upfront digital intellectual-property licensing/catalog business designed to reach first revenue quickly and then compound through reusable commercial assets, automated distribution, fulfillment, analytics, and winner expansion.

## Operating constraints
- No Jarvis integration.
- No paid acquisition or upfront investment unless explicitly approved later.
- No custom client work.
- No sales calls or routine customer interaction.
- Prioritize first real revenue over feature completeness.
- Validate demand before producing large catalogs.
- Use only original or properly licensed material; never copy competitors' protected content.
- Human involvement limited to exceptions, approvals that cannot be automated, and periodic financial review.

## Fast-track strategy
1. Demand discovery and opportunity scoring.
2. Produce a small MVP catalog of commercial micro-assets.
3. Publish through zero/low-upfront distribution channels with automated delivery/payment where available.
4. Track views, saves, conversion, revenue, refunds and support burden.
5. Kill weak products quickly; expand proven winners into adjacent SKUs.
6. Add licensing tiers and bundles only after individual products demonstrate demand.

## Initial asset categories
- Industry-specific operating toolkits
- Business calculators and decision tools
- Checklists and workflow systems
- Training/onboarding packs
- Planning/forecasting tools
- Commercial templates where they solve a specific business problem

Avoid generic planners, generic prompt packs, ebooks, and undifferentiated consumer printables.

## Revenue gates
- Gate 1: first sale
- Gate 2: $100 cumulative revenue
- Gate 3: $500/month run rate
- Gate 4: $1,000/month run rate
- Gate 5: $2,500/month run rate
- Gate 6: $5,000/month run rate

## Definition of success
A system that continues discovering, producing, testing, distributing, delivering and optimizing assets with minimal owner intervention, while generating positive net cash flow.
